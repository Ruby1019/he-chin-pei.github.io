Responsible and Auditable AI for Human-Centred Decision Support

My research focuses on how risk, uncertainty, explainability, and human oversight can support appropriate reliance on AI in healthcare and other high-stakes decision environments.

I am a Research Assistant at the Center for Neuropsychiatric Research, National Health Research Institutes, Taiwan. My work connects responsible AI, human-AI decision support, and digital health, including healthcare data interoperability and workflow design.

I completed my M.S. in Information Management at National Taipei University of Nursing and Health Sciences in July 2026. My thesis integrated process mining and simulation to evaluate and redesign carbon-emission audit workflows.
