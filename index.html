Profile

Building transparent AI decision support for real workflows.

My research focuses on AI governance, explainable AI, auditable decision support, process analytics, and responsible AI. I am particularly interested in how professionals interpret, review, question, and revise AI generated recommendations in high stakes workflows, especially when institutional accountability and human oversight matter.
