Profile

Researching transparent, auditable, and operationally useful AI.

From undergraduate study in Business Technology to graduate training in Information Management, I have become increasingly interested in the structure behind data, workflows, and decisions. Rather than only building systems, I aim to design analytical methods that are transparent, verifiable, and useful for real-world decision support.
