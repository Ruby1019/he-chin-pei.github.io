Chin-Pei He

I am a Research Assistant at the Center for Neuropsychiatric Research, National Health Research Institutes, Taiwan. My research focuses on responsible and auditable AI for human-centred decision support, with interests in explainability, risk, uncertainty, and human oversight in healthcare and other high-stakes decision environments.

I earned my M.S. in Information Management from National Taipei University of Nursing and Health Sciences in July 2026, supervised by Prof. Kuo-Chung Chu. My thesis integrated process mining and simulation to evaluate and redesign carbon-emission audit workflows. My current work includes healthcare data interoperability and FHIR-based data mapping.
