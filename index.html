Chin-Pei He

I work on explainable and auditable AI for accountable decision support. My research asks how AI systems can support high-stakes organisational decisions while remaining understandable, reviewable, traceable, and accountable.

My current thesis studies carbon-emission audit process optimization using process mining and simulation. I am especially interested in workflow transparency, bottleneck analysis, risk-oriented triage, AI governance, and responsible decision support in healthcare and institutional settings.
